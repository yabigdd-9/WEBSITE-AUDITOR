## Problem Statement

Butterfield Bathrooms has a standout asset — a Bathroom Calculator that helps homeowners get an early price guide by selecting fixtures and finishes. This is exactly the kind of lead magnet that converts visitors into enquiries. But the calculator is a standalone tool with no automation behind it:

1. **Calculator doesn't capture leads** — Users can use the calculator without providing contact details. No structured lead capture from this valuable traffic source.

2. **No follow-up automation** — Calculator users and contact form submissions are likely handled manually. No automated acknowledgement, no lead qualification workflow, no follow-up sequence.

3. **No online booking** — Consultation booking (a key step in their 4-step process) is manual. No "Book your site visit" calendar.

4. **No customer portal** — Clients have no self-service access to project updates, photos, or communication history during their renovation.

5. **Limited quote workflow** — Quotes are prepared manually after site visits. No structured process for turning calculator data + site visit notes into a formatted quote.

## Proposed Solution

### Package: Growth — Calculator Enhancement + Lead Automation

**Phase 1 — Calculator Enhancement (1-2 weeks, estimated $1,500-$2,500)**

- Add lead capture to calculator: name, phone, email before showing results (or optional email for results)
- Enhance calculator inputs: budget range, timeline, suburb, project type (new bathroom, renovation, repair)
- Add "Send me this estimate" functionality — capture lead + send PDF/email summary
- Connect calculator leads to a simple CRM or database

**Phase 2 — Lead Automation (2-3 weeks, estimated $2,000-$3,500)**

- Automatic lead acknowledgement email: "Thanks — we've received your enquiry. Marcia's team will be in touch within 24 hours."
- Lead qualification workflow: route high-priority leads (budget > $20k, timeline < 3 months) for faster response
- Follow-up sequence: reminder at 3 days, 7 days, 14 days for unconverted leads
- Quote preparation workflow: structured form capturing site visit notes, client preferences, fixture selections — generates formatted quote document

**Phase 3 — Customer Portal (optional, 2-3 weeks, estimated $2,000-$3,500)**

- Client login for project updates
- Photo gallery of their renovation progress
- Message history with the team
- Document access (care manual, warranty info, fixture specifications)

**Estimated total range:** $5,500-$9,500 + optional monthly support
