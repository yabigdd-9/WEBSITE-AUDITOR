## Problem Statement

BA Heat Pumps has a solid foundation — a Wellington-based HVAC business with 0800 number, free in-home consultation offer, interest-free payments, and genuine customer testimonials. But the website has significant gaps that likely cost them leads:

1. **No pricing transparency** — Zero prices or ranges anywhere on the site. Price-conscious shoppers bounce when they can't get a ballpark figure. Competitor AES publishes "$3,000–$8,000 including installation."

2. **Only 2 on-site testimonials** — No Google Reviews, Trustpilot, or Facebook reviews widget. Competitors with 50+ Google reviews convert better.

3. **No online booking** — Customers must call/text/email for every touchpoint. No booking calendar, no "Schedule your free assessment in 30 seconds."

4. **Manual lead capture** — The 027 mobile number and quotes@ email go to personal channels with no CRM, no automated follow-up, no lead scoring.

5. **Missing trust signals** — No master electrician registration, no ECA/MAANZ badges, no "licensed and insured" wording, no team photos, no case studies.

---

## Proposed Solution

### Package: Growth — Website + Conversion + Automation

**Phase 1 — Quick Wins (1-2 weeks, estimated $1,500-$2,500)**

- Add pricing ranges to homepage and service pages ("Single room heat pump from $2,500 installed")
- Add "Licensed & Insured" wording + master electrician/ECANZ badges (if applicable — verify first)
- Add sticky mobile "Text for Quote" / "Call Now" floating button
- Add interest-free payment messaging to every service page
- Fix or remove broken about-us page

**Phase 2 — Conversion Upgrade (2-3 weeks, estimated $2,500-$4,000)**

- Add online booking calendar (Calendly/Cal.com embed) — "Book your free assessment in 30 seconds"
- Build review generation system: post-install SMS/email asking for Google review + display widget on site
- Add before/after gallery or case study section (3-5 projects with photos)
- Add model comparison content: "Fujitsu vs Daikin vs Gree" + "What size heat pump for your room"

**Phase 3 — Automation (2-3 weeks, estimated $2,000-$3,500)**

- Lead capture system: structured enquiry form with routing to CRM (HubSpot Free or Google Sheets + Zapier)
- Automated SMS/email acknowledgement: "Thanks — Beni will be in touch within 2 hours"
- Lead scoring and follow-up sequence
- Review request automation: post-install review SMS with direct Google link

**Phase 4 — Recurring Revenue (ongoing, estimated $500-$1,000 setup + $50-$100/month)**

- "BA Comfort Plan" — annual maintenance subscription (2x services + priority booking + repair discount)
- Customer portal for service history and booking (optional phase 2)

**Estimated total range:** $6,500-$10,000 + optional monthly support
