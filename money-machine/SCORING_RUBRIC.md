# MoneyMachine Opportunity Scoring Rubric

**Version:** 2026-09-08  
**Purpose:** Score NZ business digital-improvement opportunities 0–100

---

## Scoring Factors (each scored 0–10, then weighted)

| Factor | Weight | What to assess |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem severity** | 25% | How bad is the current digital/conversion/process situation? 0 = fine, 10 = severely broken / bleeding leads |
| **Business value of fix** | 20% | How much commercial value would the improvement create? More leads, higher close rate, time saved, fewer lost enquiries |
| **Ability to pay** | 15% | Business size, revenue signals, project scale, existing spend. Can they afford a NZD 1k–10k project? |
| **Implementation difficulty** | 10% | *Inverse* — 10 = easy/cheap to deliver, 0 = massive complex rebuild. Easier = better score. |
| **Recurring-revenue potential** | 10% | Can this lead to ongoing maintenance, retainer, or repeat work? |
| **Likelihood of buying** | 10% | Do they have a visible problem they'd care about? Urgency? Decision-maker accessibility? |
| **Evidence quality** | 10% | How solid is the evidence? Live site, real reviews, business registry, current info vs. speculation |

---

## Score Bands

| Band | Range | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A+ | 80–100 | Strong, evidence-backed opportunity. Prioritize outreach. |
| A | 70–79 | Good opportunity. Worth pursuing with specific offer. |
| B | 55–69 | Real but lower-impact or harder sell. Consider if capacity allows. |
| C | 40–54 | Weak. Reject or park. |
| D | 0–39 | Not worth pursuing. Reject. |

---

## Audit Categories (each gets a 0–10 sub-score + written notes)

### Website Audit
1. **Mobile quality** — mobile layout, tap targets, readability, speed on phone
2. **Conversion quality** — CTAs, enquiry paths, friction, clarity of offer
3. **Quote flow** — is there an online quote? Instant estimate? Or phone-only?
4. **Booking flow** — can customers book online? Calendar? Or phone/email only?
5. **SEO basics** — title tags, meta, headings, content quality, local SEO
6. **Trust signals** — reviews, testimonials, associations, years in business, portfolio
7. **Page speed** — load time, performance signals (where measurable)
8. **Accessibility** — contrast, alt text, form labels, keyboard nav (where observable)
9. **Broken paths** — 404s, dead links, missing pages (especially high-intent pages)

### Backend / Automation Audit
10. **Follow-up quality** — automated confirmation? nurture sequences? or silence after enquiry?
11. **CRM signal** —CRM in use? leads tracked? or sticky notes / spreadsheets / nothing?
12. **Automation opportunities** — what manual processes could be automated? (count + describe)

### Commercial Translation
For each scored opportunity, write:
- **Problem:** specific, evidence-backed
- **Business impact:** what it costs them (lost leads, time, credibility)
- **Proposed solution:** specific scope, not generic "we make websites"
- **Commercial value:** estimated value of the improvement

---

## How to use
1. Verify the business first (separate step).
2. Score each category 0–10 with evidence.
3. Compute weighted total → 0–100.
4. Write the commercial translation.
5. Judge reviews independently.
6. Proofer checks facts before it reaches the final list.
