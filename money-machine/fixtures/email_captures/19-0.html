Fendalton Construction provides comprehensive building services throughout the Wellington region.


Fendalton Construction undertakes all aspects of the building process, including home renovations, extensions, additions, new homes, bathroom and kitchen renovations and detailed joinery, carpentry, general building work, leaky home repairs, seismic strengthening and strata maintenance. Our team of Wellington based, specialist trained builders, tradesmen and licensed building practitioners incorporates a wide range of services by providing clients with the convenience of finding all building and relevant trade requirements in one place. Contact our friendly team to discuss your ideas today.



BUILDING

The professional licensed building practitioners at Fendalton Construction offer complete building services throughout the Wellington region. Every possible type of building work is available, from bathroom and kitchen renovations to full new builds and reconstructions.




RECLADDING FOR LEAKY HOMES

If you suspect you have a leaky home contact Fendalton Construction immediately. The experienced team can diagnose your problem and recommend leaky home repairs to make your house safe and livable again. All repairs are possible from small plaster fixes to full recladding.




LAND DEVELOPMENT

Solutions for land development projects in the Wellington region are possible when you employ the expert services of Fendalton Construction. A complete land development project management package means you don't have to worry about compliance paperwork or construction matters.
