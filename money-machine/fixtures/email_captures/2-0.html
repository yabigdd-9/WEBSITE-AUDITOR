The Blizzard Experience

Blizzard HVAC & Electrical is a Wellington-based HVAC company specialising in heat pump installation, ducted air conditioning, ventilation, hot water heat pump installation, and electrical services for residential and commercial properties across Wellington, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, Porirua, Wairarapa and Kapiti Coast.

With years of industry experience, we deliver high-quality heat pump installations, air conditioning installations, and energy-efficient solutions tailored to each project. Frequently Asked Questions About Heat Pumps & Air Conditioning in Wellington

How much does it cost to install a heat pump in Wellington?

Costs vary depending on the size of the system and your home, but we provide tailored quotes to ensure you get the right solution for your space and budget.

What type of heat pump is best for my home?

The best system depends on your home layout, insulation, and heating requirements. We assess your space and recommend the most suitable option, whether it’s a high wall or ducted system.

Do you service areas outside Wellington?

Yes, we provide heat pump, air conditioning, and electrical services across Wellington, Lower Hutt, Upper Hutt, Porirua, Wairarapa and the Kapiti Coast.

Do you offer servicing and maintenance for heat pumps?

Yes, we have a dedicated service team of technicians that provide ongoing servicing, maintenance, and repairs to keep your system running efficiently and extend its lifespan.

How long does it take to install a heat pump or ducted system?

Most standard heat pump installations can be completed in a day, while ducted systems may take longer depending on the size and complexity of the project.

Can you help with both HVAC and electrical work?

Yes, we offer a full service including heat pump installation, air conditioning, ventilation, hot water heat pump and electrical services, so you don’t need to coordinate multiple contractors.

Do you provide quotes for new builds and renovations?

Yes, we work on new builds, renovations, and upgrades, providing system design and installation tailored to your project.
