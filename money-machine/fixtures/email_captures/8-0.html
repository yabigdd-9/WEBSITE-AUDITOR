Good to know

Common questions

How much does heat pump installation cost in Auckland?

Pricing depends on the unit size, the room or area it needs to heat and cool, and how complex the install is — a simple back-to-back install costs less than long pipe runs or two-storey work. We give free, fixed quotes so you know the exact price before any work starts. No surprises on the day.

Which heat pump brands do you supply and install?

We supply and install Panasonic, Daikin and Mitsubishi Electric systems — proven brands with strong manufacturer warranties that handle New Zealand conditions well. We'll recommend the right unit and size for your space, not just the most expensive one.

How long does a heat pump installation take?

A standard high-wall split system installation is usually completed in a single day, often within a few hours. Ducted and multi-room systems take longer depending on the size of the job — we'll confirm the timeframe in your quote.

Do you service all of Auckland?

Yes — we work Auckland-wide, covering Central Auckland, East Auckland, South Auckland, West Auckland and the North Shore, for both residential and commercial jobs.

Do you repair and service existing heat pumps?

Yes. We diagnose and repair faults on most major brands, and offer servicing to keep your system running efficiently — a serviced heat pump uses less power and lasts longer. If a unit isn't worth repairing, we'll tell you straight and quote a replacement.
