About Auckland Energy Solutions

The local team behind 12,500+ installs since 2014.

Auckland Energy Solutions is a Kiwi-owned family business based in Mount Wellington, Auckland. Over 12,500 installs completed for homes and businesses across Auckland, Waikato and Northland.

Our story

A family business that started with a few good electricians

Auckland Energy Solutions began in 2014 as a small team of registered electricians working out of Mount Wellington. Word spread, work grew, and today we’ve completed over 12,500 installs across heat pumps, ducted systems, hot water, ventilation, solar, and electrical upgrades for homes and businesses right across Auckland, Waikato and Northland.

We’ve stayed a family business on purpose. It keeps standards high, keeps us accountable, and means the people who quote your job are usually the same people who follow it through to install and support you for years after. Most of our work comes from referrals, so we don’t get to disappear once the invoice is paid.

Common questions

About AES: common questions

How long has Auckland Energy Solutions been around?
Since 2014. We started as a small team of registered electricians in Mount Wellington and have grown into a full-service energy solutions business. Over 12,500 installs completed across Auckland, Waikato and Northland to date.
Are you registered and accredited?

Yes. Our team includes NZ-registered electricians and manufacturer-accredited installers for the brands we install (Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Panasonic, Fujitsu General, Carrier, Haier). Registration and accreditation matter because they mean warranties stay valid and the work meets the standards it’s supposed to.

What brands do you install?

For heat pumps and air conditioning we’re authorised for Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin, Panasonic, Fujitsu, Carrier and Haier. For hot water heat pumps: Haier, Panasonic, and Mitsubishi AquaCore. For ventilation: Mitsubishi Electric Lossnay. We chose these because they cover every household type and price point, and because their warranty and support are actually reliable.

Where are you based and where do you work?
Our office and warehouse are in Mount Wellington, Auckland. We install across the whole of Auckland, most of Waikato, and Northland. Not sure if we cover your area? Just ask when you enquire, and we’ll let you know straight away.
Do you do commercial work as well as residential?

Yes. We handle business fit-outs, commercial ducted systems, larger heat pump installs for offices and retail, solar for commercial roofs, EV charging infrastructure, and general electrical work. If you’re in a commercial or industrial space and need heating, cooling, ventilation, solar or electrical, we can quote.

How do I get started?
Book a free in-home assessment. One of our senior team visits, spends about 45 minutes looking at your space and how you use it, then walks you through the options in plain English. You get a fixed-price quote to keep. No pressure, no obligation. Whether you go with us or not, you’ll have honest advice to work with.

Ready to work with a team that shows up?

One of our senior team visits, looks at your home properly, and designs the right system for it. The design and quote are yours to keep — whether you go ahead with us or not.
