**Subject:** Quick idea for your heat pump site — pricing + booking

Hi Beni,

I was looking at baheatpumps.co.nz and wanted to share a couple of observations.

First — your testimonials are genuine and specific (Lance's installation timeline, Catherine's experience). That's the kind of social proof that converts. But they're buried on the contact page rather than on the homepage where price-shoppers would see them.

Second — there's no pricing anywhere on the site. Not even a "from $X" range. When someone searches "heat pump Wellington" and lands on your site, they have no way to tell if you're in their budget. I noticed AES (another Auckland installer) publishes "$3,000–$8,000 including installation" right on their site — that kind of transparency captures the comparison shoppers before they bounce.

A couple of specific ideas:

1. **Add pricing ranges** to your homepage and service pages. Even a rough "Single room heat pump from $2,500 installed" would help. You can always say "final quote after assessment" — but giving people a ballpark keeps them engaged.

2. **Put a booking calendar on your contact page.** Your "free in-home consultation" is a strong offer — let people book it in 30 seconds instead of texting/calling back and forth.

3. **Get your Google reviews on the site.** Even a simple widget showing your review count and average would help. Most customers check Google before calling.

4. **Add a sticky "Text for Quote" button** on mobile so people scrolling past the header can still easily reach you.

None of this requires a rebuild — these are targeted additions to your existing site.
