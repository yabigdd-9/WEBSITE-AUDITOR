**Subject:** Idea for your bathroom calculator — capture the leads it's already generating

Hi Butterfield team,

I was on your site looking at the bathroom calculator — it's a genuinely useful tool. Being able to get an early price guide by selecting fixtures and finishes is exactly what people want before they commit to a site visit.

One thing I noticed: the calculator doesn't ask for contact details before showing results. That means you're getting traffic from people seriously considering a renovation, but you might not be capturing their details to follow up.

A couple of ideas:

1. **Add a simple "Get this estimate emailed to me" option** — name, phone, email. People who want the estimate sent to them are high-intent leads. Those contact details put you in a much stronger position to follow up.

2. **Automate the first response.** When someone submits their calculator results or contact form, an automatic email acknowledging their enquiry and setting expectations ("Marcia's team will be in touch within 24 hours") makes a strong first impression and takes pressure off manual follow-up.

3. **Add an online booking option for site visits.** Your 4-step process starts with a site visit — letting people book that directly on the site (instead of calling/emailing back and forth) would reduce friction.

The calculator is already a differentiator — most bathroom renovators don't have anything like it. Connecting it to lead capture and follow-up would make it even more valuable.

If any of this sounds relevant, happy to have a quick chat. No pressure.
