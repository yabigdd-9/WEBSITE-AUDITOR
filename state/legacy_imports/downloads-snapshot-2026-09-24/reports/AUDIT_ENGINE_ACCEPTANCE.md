# WEBSITES/BUISNESSaudits — audit acceptance

PASS for the bounded evidence schema and internal packet path. Broader business operations remain NEEDS_CONFIRMATION.

Findings carry ID, category, P0/P1/P2 severity, observed evidence, source URL/hash/date, impact, confidence, recommendation, effort, dependency, demo eligibility and commercial hypothesis. Static absence is distinguished from visible behavior. Missing labels cannot qualify an offer until matched to a hash-bound restricted browser review. Hidden upload/anti-spam fields and hidden duplicate-name controls are excluded from confirmed claims. Missing viewport observations remain NEEDS_CONFIRMATION; missing meta descriptions are bounded HTML facts, not revenue claims.

The existing detector now treats TLS/network uncertainty, 403 and timeouts as unknowns, rather than proof of broken HTTPS/links. Only 404/410 count as broken targets. Unsupported claims about lost enquiries or abandonment were removed.

All 30 required website/operations dimensions are represented. Most have no direct observation and remain NEEDS_CONFIRMATION: analytics, CRM, revenue, buying capacity, booking outcomes, privacy/legal compliance and internal workflows are not inferred from a homepage. The pilot is a focused field-label improvement, not an accessibility certification or complete business audit.

The 11-dimension qualification scorecard uses 0–5 with explanations; unknown buying value, decision-maker access and margin are null. A verified problem and HIGH website identity are required; an email alone fails. Buying capacity remains unknown. The offer maps only to confirmed P1 findings, defines exclusions/assumptions/acceptance, and leaves price unset.

The Moutere contact page has two visible controls without associated names in the restricted check: search and message. Aspect has one visible address field. Third-party scripts were blocked, so owner confirmation on the full rendered site is still required. No forms were submitted. Revenue impact remains unmeasured.

Browser acceptance: both local demos at 360px and 1280px have associated labels, no horizontal overflow, keyboard focus, working preview, zero external requests and no script errors. See demo-browser-tests.json and preview images. Regression tests reject tampered captures, tampered rendered DOM, fabricated results, static-only/hidden controls, unsupported evidence and altered approval states.

Evidence is in the sibling outputs/evidence directory. All times in evidence are UTC; this run occurred 13 September 2026 in Pacific/Auckland.
