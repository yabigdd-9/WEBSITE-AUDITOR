# Safe legal first-contact plan

This is a compliant, execution-ready launch plan for the Hermes Money Engine. It does not send any email, publish outreach, or bypass the repo's approval consent gates.

## Operating rule

- No cold email from public contact data alone.
- NZBN may be used to verify entity identity.
- Email is only permitted after explicit opt-in, clear reply, or prior documented relationship.
- Phone or form-first contact is the default fast path.
- Every contact must carry a written evidence record.

## 1. Immediate approval gates

Before any lead is contacted:

1. APR-004: final pricing band approved
2. APR-006: scope ratification approved
3. APR-007: per-prospect consent rationale reviewed and signed off

If these are not complete, the lead remains in observation / no-send state.

## 2. Fast legal contact path

### Step A: verify the company

For each target, record:

- business name
- NZBN
- website URL
- exact contact path used
- business category
- match to service offering
- any refusal statements or anti-marketing language

### Step B: prefer phone or form first

Use one of the following as the first touch:

- phone call to the business
- website contact form
- lead magnet / quote request / free audit form
- direct reply path with an opt-in checkbox

This is the lowest-risk acceleration path.

### Step C: only send email after proof

Send email only if one of these is true:

- the business explicitly opted in
- the business replied to the first contact and invited follow-up
- there is a valid earlier relationship that is documented and relevant

Anything else stays blocked.

## 3. Required proof record per lead

For each lead, keep a single record with:

- company_name
- nzbn
- website_url
- contact_channel_used
- date_contacted
- reason_for_contact
- evidence_url_used
- any_refusal_statement
- response_received
- consent_status
- human_rated_by
- ratified_at

If any required fact is missing, the lead is not eligible for email.

## 4. Mail content rules

If email is allowed:

- truthful sender identity
- real reply address and phone
- clear reason for contacting this specific business
- unsubscribe or opt-out path that works
- no pricing if APR-004 is still open
- no misleading claims or unverifiable statements

## 5. Small-batch launch plan

Day 1:
- lock pricing approval and contact SOP

Day 2:
- verify a shortlist with NZBN

Day 3:
- call or form-first contact for 10-20 qualified leads

Day 4:
- log interactions and review the evidence bundle

Day 5:
- convert warm replies into opt-in follow-up or a short email

Day 6:
- measure responses and refine the offer

Day 7:
- continue only with the leads that have documented positive engagement

## 6. Hard stop rules

Do not proceed if:

- public business email is treated as consent
- there is no written proof record
- a refusal / anti-marketing statement is present
- the lead is from a harvested list or scraped data source
- the message is generic and not business-specific

## 7. Safe execution note

This plan is intended to accelerate real outreach without bypassing the consent gate. The repo's governing principle remains: publication is not permission, and the burden of proof sits with the sender.
