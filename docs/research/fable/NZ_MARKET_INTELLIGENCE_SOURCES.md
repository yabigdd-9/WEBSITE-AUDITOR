# NZ Market Intelligence Sources Research

This document summarizes research on public NZ datasets that can improve opportunity context without revealing individual purchase intent.

## Sources to Assess
- Stats NZ Business Demography
- regional enterprise counts
- business births/deaths
- employment by industry
- building consents
- construction activity
- population/business density
- other relevant government sector statistics

## Classification of Signals
Each signal is classified as:
- MARKET_SIZE
- MARKET_GROWTH
- MARKET_DENSITY
- VERTICAL_DEMAND_CONTEXT
- NOT_INDIVIDUAL_INTENT

## Next Steps
- Evaluate each source for accessibility, license, cost, and commercial-use constraints.
- Determine which signals can be safely integrated without violating privacy constraints.
- Develop metrics for market context that are aggregated and anonymized.
