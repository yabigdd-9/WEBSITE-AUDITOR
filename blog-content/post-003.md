## How to Read a Nutrient Label: NPK Numbers Explained

Every nutrient bottle has 3 big numbers. They're the NPK ratio — Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium. But most labels hide what actually matters.

### What the Numbers Mean
- **First number (N):** Leaf and stem growth. Higher = more vegetation.
- **Second number (P):** Roots, flowers, fruit. Higher = better flowering.
- **Third number (K):** Overall plant health, disease resistance, water uptake.

### Example Comparison

| Product | N-P-K | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| A-X PRO | 5-3-4 | Vegetative growth |
| PK-X | 2-8-6 | Flowering/fruiting |
| MICRO-X | 1-1-1 + trace elements | All stages (micro-nutrients) |

### The NZ Trap
Most NZ nutrient brands use "covering" NPK numbers that look impressive but don't tell you about bioavailability. CATALYX formulas are chelated — meaning your plants actually absorb the nutrients, not just flush them through.

1. **Chelated micronutrients** — EDTA or DTPA chelation means better uptake
2. **pH range** — CATALYX works at 5.5-6.5 (wide range for NZ water)
3. **EC levels** — how strong the solution is (CATALYX: 1.2-2.4 mS/cm)
4. **Shelf life** — opened bottles last 6-12 months

### Quick Test: Compare Two Bottles
1. Look at the NPK ratio
2. Check if micronutrients are listed separately or "included"
3. Look for chelation method
4. Check recommended EC range
