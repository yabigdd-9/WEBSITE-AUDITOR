## The 5 Most Common Nutrient Deficiencies in NZ Hydroponic Tomatoes

New Zealand's climate is brutal on hydroponic tomatoes — cold nights, variable light, and soil that leaches minerals fast. Most growers lose 20-40% of their yield to nutrient issues they never diagnose.

Here are the 5 deficiencies that kill NZ hydro tomatoes, ranked by frequency, with the exact CATALYX fix for each.

### 1. Nitrogen Deficiency (Most Common)
**Symptoms:** Yellowing lower leaves, stunted growth, pale green new foliage.
**Why NZ:** Cold soil slows nitrogen uptake. Your roots can't absorb N even when it's in the solution.
**Fix:** Increase MICRO-X dose by 20% during vegetative stage. Check pH — N locks out below 5.5.

### 2. Calcium Deficiency
**Symptoms:** Blossom end rot, curled young leaves, dark spots on fruit.
**Why NZ:** Rainwater dilutes calcium. NZ growers using tank/rain water see this constantly.
**Fix:** Add TRACE-X at 2ml/L. Never mix calcium with phosphorus at high doses — stagger feeding.

### 3. Phosphorus Deficiency
**Symptoms:** Purple stems, dark green leaves, slow flowering.
**Why NZ:** Cold roots (below 18°C) can't take up P. NZ autumn/winter grows hit this hard.
**Fix:** PK-X at full dose during flowering. Warm your reservoir to 20-22°C.

### 4. Potassium Deficiency
**Symptoms:** Brown leaf edges, weak fruit development, yellowing between veins.
**Why NZ:** High rainfall leaches K from soil and grow media.
**Fix:** RIPEN-X (when ready) or PK-X boost at week 3-4 of flower cycle.

### 5. Magnesium Deficiency
**Symptoms:** Yellowing between leaf veins, reddish stems.
**Why NZ:** Coconut coir binds magnesium aggressively.
**Fix:** FLUSH-X reset, then MICRO-X at 1.5x standard dose for 1 week.
