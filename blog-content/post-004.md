## NZ Winter Growing Guide: Feeding in Cold Weather

NZ winters kill more grow cycles than pests do. Not because of the cold directly — because growers keep feeding the same schedule they used in summer. Your plants' roots can't absorb nutrients when the solution is cold.

### The Problem
Below 15°C, nutrient uptake drops by 30-50%. Above 25°C, oxygen drops and roots suffocate. The sweet spot: 18-22°C.

### Winter Feeding Adjustments

**Reduce doses by 20-30%** — cold roots can't process full-strength nutrients. Burn risk is higher because uptake slows but you're still feeding.

**Switch to MICRO-X as base** — trace elements are more critical in winter when growth slows. Plants need micronutrients more than macronutrients.

**Warm your reservoir** — a $30 aquarium heater keeps solution at 20°C. This single change eliminates most winter deficiencies.

**Cut back on PK-X** — flowering slows in cold. Don't force it. Focus on keeping plants alive and healthy.

### Extra Tips
- Check pH daily — cold water holds more CO2, which drops pH
- Top up with plain water, not nutrients — evaporation is slower in winter
- Use a TDS meter — if EC creeps above 2.5, flush and restart

**Winter growing isn't about more food. It's about less food, warmer water, and patience.**
