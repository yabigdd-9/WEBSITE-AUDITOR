### How long does a kitchen renovation usually take?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. Smaller renovations may take a few weeks, while larger projects could span several months. We give you a realistic timeline upfront.

It depends on the scope of work. For major projects, it can be more comfortable to move out temporarily. Smaller renovations can often be completed while you remain in your home. We talk this through with you before work starts.
