## Roofing Built for Wellington’s Wind

Wellington is one of the windiest cities in the world, and wind is the number one enemy of a roof here. High winds create uplift that can lift poorly fixed sheets, tear off ridge cappings, and work flashings loose over time. A roof that would be fine in a sheltered inland town needs more here: correct fixing patterns, wind-rated detailing, and materials specified for high-wind zones.

This is where getting the install right matters most. Roofing Dept builds Wellington roofs to handle the conditions, with the fixing density and detailing the wind demands. It is the difference between a roof that lasts and one that fails in the first big southerly.

### What roofing is best for Wellington's wind?

Wellington’s high winds demand correct fixing patterns, wind-rated detailing, and materials specified for high-wind zones. Longrun metal, properly fixed, performs well. The key is the quality of the installation, which is where Roofing Dept focuses.

### Why do Wellington roofs fail more often?

Wellington’s extreme wind creates uplift that lifts poorly fixed sheets, tears cappings, and works flashings loose. Roofs that were not installed to handle high-wind conditions fail earlier here than almost anywhere in New Zealand.
