## Heat Pump Installation FAQ

1. How long does installation take?

Most residential installations take a half-day. More complex setups may require a full day depending on access and system type.

2. What’s included in the installation?

Everything needed to get your system working — mounting, cabling, electrical connections, refrigerant piping, testing, and setup.

3. Do I need to be home during the install?

Yes, ideally at the start and end. We’ll walk you through positioning, handover, and how to get the best from your unit.

4. Can you install in apartments or compact homes?

Yes — we regularly work in apartments, units, and townhouses. We can also coordinate with building managers or body corps if needed.

5. Do you supply the heat pump as well?

Yes, we can supply and install. We also install customer-supplied units if they meet NZ compliance standards.

6. What brands do you recommend?

We primarily install Mitsubishi Electric and Haier systems for their proven performance and local support in NZ.

7. Can you install multi-room or ducted systems?

Absolutely — we work with a wide range of layouts and can design systems for larger homes or open-plan offices.
