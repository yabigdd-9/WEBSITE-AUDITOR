## Give your kitchen a much-needed facelift with all new doors, panels or a few extra cupboards

If your kitchen layout still works well but the space is starting to look tired or outdated, a kitchen makeover or kitchen facelift can completely refresh the look and feel of your home without the cost of a full kitchen renovation. Dstone Kitchens provides customised kitchen upgrades and kitchen remodel solutions that modernise your existing space while making better use of the cabinetry you already have.
...
By retaining your current kitchen framework and replacing selected components, we can transform the appearance of your kitchen with new doors, panels, drawer fronts, handles and updated finishes. We can also refresh toe kicks with thin overlays, helping older kitchens look clean, modern and revitalised again.
...
As part of your kitchen upgrade, we can identify opportunities to improve storage and functionality by adding extra cupboards, overhead cabinetry or additional drawers to maximise your available space. If your budget allows, we also offer selected stone benchtop options to replace older laminate surfaces and complete the transformation.
