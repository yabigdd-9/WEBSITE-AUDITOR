The company

Reliable plumbing for local homes and small businesses.

Founded by Wade Mitchell, Mitchell Plumbers Ltd is a Wellington company that believes in doing it once and doing it right. We turn up, talk it through, and keep your place tidy.

We look for the cause, not just the symptom. You get clear options, fair pricing, and workmanship we are happy to put our name on.
