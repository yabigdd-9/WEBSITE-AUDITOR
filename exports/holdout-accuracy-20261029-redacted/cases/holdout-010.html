Hi, I'm Gav the director of Gavin Loye Plumbing. I started this business out of my home office back in 1983, when I had become qualified in plumbing, drainage, and gas fitting and was ready for my next challenge. With 12 staff, seven service vehicles, two trucks, and two diggers, Gavin Loye Plumbing is all about getting the job done.  Perfectly located in Hamilton on Killarney Road, Frankton, we can attend to your plumbing needs anywhere in Hamilton and the greater Waikato.


About:

GLP's team of qualified and reliable tradesmen offer a wide range of services such as plumbing, drainlaying, gasfitting, solar, roof repairs and maintenance, wastewater management systems, and solid fuel fire installation.  Gavin Loye, the company owner and director, is a qualified Craftsman/certifying Plumber, Gasfitter, Drainlayer, and Waste Water System Designer.

Since 1983, GLP's customer base and staff has grown which created the need to move from it's home based office in Tamahere into it's current premises on Killarney Road in Frankton, Hamilton. 

Having worked with various HCC inspectors and engineers on past projects, GLP Ltd staff are aware of HCC protocol and boundaries. As members of Hazard Co, GLP Ltd is a safety conscious company who creates safer working environments for all staff, clients, and sub contractors.

GLP Ltd have been the preferred maintenance contractor for St Peters Cambridge for numerous years and have recently been responsible for installing and advising on a new water reticulation system which was completed to a very high standard.
