Switchboard upgrades

Is your switchboard still on ceramic fuses?

A lot of Christchurch homes are still running boards that predate modern safety requirements. Without RCD protection, a fault can energise anything a person is touching before the circuit ever trips.

An upgrade gives you RCD protection on your circuits, capacity for the loads a modern household actually runs, and a board that can be safely worked on and clearly labelled.
