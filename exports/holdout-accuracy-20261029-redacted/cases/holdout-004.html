    Eco Friendly Building

    Our Services

Excelsior are professional home builders serving Hamilton and the wider Waikato, constructing homes that reflect your individuality. We are leaders in sustainable builds.

When building your new home, our team culture of ‘building with integrity’ and ‘with a good attitude’ impacts positively on everything we do on your building site. We have a team of highly skilled tradesman with 106 years of building experience between us.

Consultation

During the initial consultation process, we discuss your hopes and ideas for your build and work out how we can achieve these in a sustainable and eco-friendly way, ensuring you can make environmentally conscious choices for your new home.

Working on site

Our Eco-sustainable house building practices focus on using using resources with minimal waste, energy-efficient designs, and environmentally friendly materials to minimize the carbon footprint and enhance long-term sustainability

Post Construction

By working in a sustainable way to build your new home, you can have the peace of mind that your new home has had the least impact possible on the planet. 

New & Sustainable Home Builders Across Hamilton & Waikato

We expect all our team members to achieve the highest level of workmanship on every project. This ensures we deliver high standards and a great finish to your home, right down to the very last nail! Working with you we offer full design and build from start to finish. Bring in the plans of your dream home, or choose from our range of certified plans and we are happy to quote and build. You can modify our certified plans to meet your personal needs. Call Peter today on 021 276 9688 to discuss your building or renovation dreams.
