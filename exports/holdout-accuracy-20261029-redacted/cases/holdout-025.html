About Irving Builders

Irving Builders aim is to build a customer focused organisation recognised not only for the high calibre of our work, but also for having a great team and a fantastic culture as our foundation.

To be the “Best We Can” comes with constant improvement, innovation and understanding clearly our “why”.

7 reasons why you should choose

IRVING BUILDERS

  • Licensed Building Practitioner Member

    We hold LPB Site 2 Licenses. All restricted building work must be overseen by a LBP. To obtain (and retain) your LPB certification you must be able to prove your capabilities as a builder and maintain a record of your ongoing learning

  • National Retail Design Award Winner

    NZ wide award won in 2011 for design function and overall quality of fitout for SoYo retail outlet in Riccarton

  • Member of NZ Institute of Building

    Both Lance and Matt are members of this multi disciplined organisation. It allows for networking and liaising with other professionals in the wider industry arena.

  • ACC Tertiary Level Qualification

    Irving Builders obtained tertiary level (the highest) quality accreditation at our first audit. This reflects our high level, and commitment to, Health and Safety over all of our sites

  • Master Builder Silver Award 2014

    Our excellence has been recognized in the past when we won the Master Builders Silver Award in the $600,000 to $1 Million Category for a New Build in Christchurch. This recognition can give you trust in our ability to deliver on your project!

  • Member of NZ Society of Construction Law

    The NZ Society of Construction Law membership enables us to monitor changes in construction law within NZ and to discuss relevant topics specific to the industry

  • Member of NZ Institute of Safety Management

    NZ Institute of Safety Managers membership provides a resource to remain up to date with Health and Safety changes within NZ. Matt is currently studying for his Diploma in Health and Safety.
