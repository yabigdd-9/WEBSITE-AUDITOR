Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know when I should call a plumber?

Contact a plumber if you notice persistent leaks, unusually low water pressure, slow drainage, unusual noises from plumbing fixtures, unexplained increases in water use or changes in your hot water supply. Addressing problems early can often help prevent further damage.

Can you work with my builder on a construction project?

Yes. JM Plumbing Solutions regularly works alongside builders and other trades on construction projects. Coordinating plumbing work with the wider building programme helps ensure installations are completed at the appropriate stage of the project.

Can you help me decide whether to repair or replace my hot water cylinder?

Yes. The best option depends on factors including the cylinder's age, condition, capacity, efficiency and the cost of repairing the existing system. We can assess the situation and explain the practical options available.

What information should I provide when requesting a plumbing quote?

It helps to provide your property location, a description of the work required and any relevant photographs. For renovation, new build or larger commercial projects, plans or specifications can also help us understand the scope of work.

Do you work on both older and newer Christchurch properties?

Yes. Christchurch has properties from many different construction periods, each with different plumbing systems and requirements. Our industry experience allows us to work with both existing plumbing systems and modern installations.
