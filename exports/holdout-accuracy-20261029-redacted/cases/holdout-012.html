A commitment to innovation, regulation, compliance, sustainability and excellence defines our journey. We are the hub for all things heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration (HVAC&R) and take pride in steering the course of an industry that shapes the air that we breathe and the spaces we inhabit.

New Zealand’s industry association for HVAC&R professionals

Nationwide industry association representing companies in the HVAC&R industry

The source of all HVAC&R industry training

The HVAC&R Centre is a key supporter of New Zealand’s HVAC&R industry, providing essential governance and operational assistance to prominent organisations such as IRHACE (Institute of Refrigeration, Heating, and Air Conditioning Engineers), CCCANZ (Climate Control Companies Association of NZ), RLNZ (Refrigerant License New Zealand), and their regional and member networks.

Collectively, this support spans over 800 individuals and 120 companies nationwide, all actively engaged in heating, ventilation, air conditioning, air quality, mechanical building services and refrigeration. Our flagship HVAC&R Hub in July in Auckland and HVAC&R South are the premier events for HVAC&R professionals across New Zealand, drawing over 8,000 visitors each year. These vibrant gatherings features industry-leading exhibitors, expert-led seminars, and valuable networking opportunities. In addition to our main events, we host the regional Wave events which are delivered through flexible models that can be tailored to suit regional, industry and educational needs.

 

New Zealand’s HVAC&R industry is vibrant and active, featuring various trade shows, networking and webinars, member activities, professional development and education training, and competitions for apprentices in the Wallie Askew Memorial Workskills Competition and representing New Zealand in WorldSkills.

Whether you’re an HVAC&R professional or a company involved in specialist trades in the construction sector, we warmly invite you to join our thriving network of members from across New Zealand.
