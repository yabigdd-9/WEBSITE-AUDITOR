About 

The Tile Association of New Zealand (TANZ) is dedicated to supporting, educating, and safeguarding the tile industry for the benefit of its members. Our key offerings include:

  • Career Advancement: Receive expert advice, support, and training to help you progress in your tiling career.
  • Business Services: Access essential resources like health and safety guidance, as well as insurance services tailored for the industry.
  • Technical Support: Stay informed with the latest industry updates, technical information, and expert advice to ensure top-quality performance.
  • Building Code Library: Access a comprehensive library of resources specific to the New Zealand building code, ensuring you comply with local regulations.
  • Free SEO-Optimized Directory Listing: Increase visibility with a complimentary listing in the TANZ directory.

International Collaboration

The Tile Association of New Zealand (TANZ) and the National Tile Contractors Association (NTCA) are affiliated industry associations, working together to support shared objectives in education, training, technical guidance, and the promotion of best practice throughout the tiling industry.

As one of the world's largest and most respected tile industry associations, the NTCA provides valuable international knowledge and technical resources that help TANZ further strengthen the New Zealand tile industry.

The NTCA 2025/2026 Reference Manual is made available by TANZ as an international reference guide for tile installation. While it shares many similarities with the TANZ Best Practice Guide, the TANZ publication has been specifically developed for New Zealand construction practices and building requirements, and is proudly endorsed by the Ministry of Business, Innovation and Employment (MBIE).

Tiler Membership

Join the Tile Association of New Zealand (TANZ) as a professional tiler and become part of a nationwide network dedicated to raising the standard of the tiling industry. As a member, you’ll gain access to valuable resources, exclusive training opportunities, and support to elevate your craft. TANZ membership ensures you are equipped with the latest industry knowledge, from best practices to regulatory changes, giving you an edge in delivering quality workmanship that sets you apart.

Take pride in your work, gain customer trust, and enjoy the benefits of being backed by a nationally recognized association.

Architect Membership

Architects play an integral role in shaping innovative and aesthetically pleasing designs, including tile installations. By becoming a TANZ member, you gain access to a network of trusted tiling professionals, as well as technical resources and industry standards that support your design and construction decisions. Whether you’re looking to stay informed on the latest in tile technology or seeking collaboration with experts who prioritize quality installation, TANZ membership gives you the backing of a respected national organization.

Join TANZ today and contribute to advancing the standards of the tiling industry.

Builder Membership

Builders are at the forefront of ensuring that every aspect of construction meets high standards, including tiling. By joining TANZ, you’ll have direct access to a community of qualified tilers, product suppliers, and industry experts who share a commitment to excellence.

As a member, you will benefit from resources that help you choose the best tiling systems and materials for your projects, ensuring compliance and long-lasting results. TANZ membership provides a valuable network and support system for builders who are serious about delivering superior craftsmanship and customer satisfaction.
