Quality Dunedin Homes for Investors and Homeowners

Award-winning TGC Homes is recognised as Dunedin's leading townhouse developer and builder, and offers a diverse range of housing solutions from entry level housing for first-home buyers, commuters and investors, to high-end homes for retirees, downsizers and young families. The company also plays a role in addressing Dunedin's significant social housing shortfall.

Passionate about creating warm, affordable homes, TGC understands that quality housing is the cornerstone of thriving communities. Every development is carefully scrutinised to ensure it provides a healthy, functional space where residents can live, work and play.

For TGC, property development is about more than just building houses; it’s about delivering the right homes, in the right locations, at the right price.

Testimonials

Purchase Direct from TGC Homes

Many of our clients purchase directly from us to benefit from a personalised service and a smooth buying experience. Our homes are typically sold before construction even begins - a process known as "buying off-the-plans." You’ll pay a deposit to lock in your purchase and then the balance of the purchase price upon completion of the build.

There can be a lot to consider when buying a home and our team is here to guide you through every stage of the buying process. We've put together a guide to help you understand the benefits and walk you through the steps.

First Home Advantage Scheme

Our First Home Advantage deal supports first-home buyers to get on the property ladder. If you’re buying your first home, you may be eligible to receive:

  • $10,000, per owner (up to 3 owners)
  • $5,000 towards furniture and fit-out
  • And you only need a $10,000 deposit to secure your new home
