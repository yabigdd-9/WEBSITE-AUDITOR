XP Electrical is your one-stop-electrical-shop offering the full suite of Industrial, Commercial and Residential services.

At XP Electrical, we are proud to be Hamilton’s trusted electricians, known for our exceptional electrical services. We offer a comprehensive range of solutions to meet all your electrical needs, including industrial, commercial, and residential projects.

Whether it’s general electrical work, complex industrial installations, or specialised commercial systems, our expertise and knowledge ensure the job is done right.

CONTACT US

Residential

Keep your home perfectly powered with our wide range of services. From new builds and renovations to general maintenance and lighting installations, we cover it all. We excel in fault finding and repairs, data, TV, and networking solutions, as well as solar design and installation. Count on us for professional, high-quality service at every step.

Commercial

For all your commercial electrical needs, rely on our precision and expertise. We focus on new office fit-outs and renovations, shop and retail fit-outs, and school and Ministry of Education projects. Enhance your space with energy-efficient LED lighting and depend on us for fault finding and repairs. Stay connected with our data and networking solutions, and trust our lighting and power design and installation services.

Industrial

Our comprehensive industrial electrical services cover general maintenance and installation works, pump station design and installation, waste and water treatment installations. We also handle factory fit-outs, sheds and warehousing, and data, telemetry, and network installations. Additionally, we are proficient in council maintenance, installation, and service work.
