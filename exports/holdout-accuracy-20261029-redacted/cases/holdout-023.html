SERVICE AREA

We have fair & open pricing

We’re always upfront and fair about the costs involved in our service. No hassles, no unjustified expenses, and no nasty surprises. We have an open book cost plus policy and keep everything transparent.

We involve you in our projects

No one likes to be kept in the dark – we maintain great communication with regular client meetings to address any concerns and to let you have your say on the finishing touches! You’ll always be kept in the loop.

Quality over quantity focused

Our work is completed to the highest standards and we are accredited to offer the Master Builders 10 Year Guarantee! We take great pride in our work and each detail is handled with precision and care.
