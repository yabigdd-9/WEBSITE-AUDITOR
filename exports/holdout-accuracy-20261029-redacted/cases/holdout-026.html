  • About us

Greater Construction Auckland specialises in high-quality new builds, renovations, and extensions designed around the way you live.

We take a hands-on approach to every project working closely with homeowners, first-time builders, and property investors to ensure clarity, precision, and confidence at every stage.

We take pride in supporting homeowners from all backgrounds, including the Pacific community, by understanding the cultural values, family priorities, and long-term vision that shape every home. Our team ensures clients feel heard, respected, and confident throughout the entire building process.
