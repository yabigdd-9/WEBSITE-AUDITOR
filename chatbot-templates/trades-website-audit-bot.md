# Website Audit Bot — Trades Businesses

**Platform:** ManyChat / Typebot
**Revenue:** $500-1000/mo per bot
**Setup Time:** 3-4 hours

## Flow
- Welcome: 'Want a free website audit?'
- Email capture
- Auto-audit: check contact page, load speed, mobile-friendliness
- Send report with defects + fix recommendations
- CTA → 'Book a call to fix it' (upsell)
- Follow-up → 7-day email sequence with case studies

## Monetization
$300-500 per audit + $200/mo maintenance retainer
