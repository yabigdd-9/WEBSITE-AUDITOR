# Hydro Product Advisor Bot — Hydro Shops

**Platform:** ManyChat / Typebot
**Revenue:** $150-400/mo per bot
**Setup Time:** 2-3 hours

## Flow
- Welcome: 'What's your setup?'
- Setup type → DWC, NFT, drip, soil
- Size → small, medium, large
- Budget → under $100, $100-500, $500+
- Goal → yield, quality, efficiency
- Recommendation → products + pricing
- CTA → 'Shop now' (affiliate link) or 'Visit store'
- Follow-up → product comparison email

Affiliate commission + $150/mo retainer from hydro shop
