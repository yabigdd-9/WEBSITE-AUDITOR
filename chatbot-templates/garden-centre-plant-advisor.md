# Plant Advisor Bot — Garden Centres

**Platform:** ManyChat / Typebot
**Revenue:** $150-400/mo per bot
**Setup Time:** 2 hours

## Flow
- Welcome: 'What are you planting?'
- Location → NZ region
- Sun exposure → full sun, partial, shade
- Skill level → beginner, intermediate, advanced
- Recommendation → plant type + care tips
- CTA → 'Check availability at our store' (drives foot traffic)
- Follow-up → care guide email sequence

## Monetization
Drive in-store sales + $150/mo retainer from garden centre
