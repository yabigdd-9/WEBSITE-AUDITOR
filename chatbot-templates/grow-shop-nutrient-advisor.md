# Nutrient Advisor Bot — NZ Grow Shops

**Platform:** ManyChat / Typebot
**Revenue:** $200-500/mo per bot
**Setup Time:** 2-3 hours

## Flow
- Welcome: 'What are you growing?'
- Crop selection → tomato, pepper, leafy, herbs, flowers
- Growth stage → seedling, veg, flower, harvest
- Setup type → hydro, soil, coco
- Recommendation → CATALYX product + dose
- CTA → 'Get your free feeding plan' (email capture)
- Follow-up → 3-day email sequence with dosing tips

## Monetization
Affiliate commission on CATALYX sales + $200/mo retainer from grow shop
